# Assistance and solution policy

Exercise implementations are intentionally absent.

Before asking for assistance, include:
- Your attempt or design sketch.
- The exact failing test and output.
- What you expected and what you have already tried.

Assistance order: clarify the contract, ask a diagnostic question, offer a hint,
review the attempt, then discuss a solution only after an attempt and an explicit request.
Keep accepted attempts in their original day folders.
If a reference solution is later requested, agree where to put it and avoid replacing the learner's work silently.

Examples demonstrate adjacent concepts; they must not become hidden exercise answers.
Architecture diagrams, ADRs, APIs and tests for harder work are learner deliverables.
Do not mark days complete merely because the starter contract passes.
